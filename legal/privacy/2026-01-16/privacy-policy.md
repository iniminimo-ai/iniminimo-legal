# Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 16, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, payment information, and photos you upload to create music videos. We also collect usage data about how you interact with our service.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve our services, process your payments, send you updates and notifications, and personalize your experience. Your photos are used solely to create your requested videos.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your photos and videos are stored securely using encrypted storage solutions. We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services.

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers who help us operate our platform, but only to the extent necessary and under strict confidentiality agreements.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Our service is intended for use by parents and guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Parents are responsible for the content they upload about their children.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can download or delete your videos at any time through your account settings. You may also request a copy of all your data.

## 7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience, analyze usage patterns, and remember your preferences. You can control cookie settings through your browser.

## 8. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services for payment processing, analytics, and video generation. These services have their own privacy policies and we encourage you to review them.

## 9. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or through the app.

## 10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at [privacy@iniminimo.ai](mailto:privacy@iniminimo.ai)
