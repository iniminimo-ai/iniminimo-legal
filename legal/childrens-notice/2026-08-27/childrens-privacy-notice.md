## 1. Who this notice is for

Iniminimo is used by parents and guardians, who create personalised videos for
a child in their care. This notice is written for that parent or guardian. It
describes, in one place, what happens to the information about a child that a
parent chooses to give us.

## 2. What is still to be written

The final notice will cover, at minimum:

- what information about a child a parent provides, and what is optional;
- what that information is used for, and for how long it is kept;
- which third parties it is sent to in order to produce a video, and what we
  send them;
- how a parent reviews, exports or deletes it;
- how a parent withdraws consent, and what happens when they do;
- who to contact, and how to complain.

## 3. Until then

Nothing in this placeholder narrows or replaces the Privacy Policy or the Terms
of Service. Where this notice is silent — which today is everywhere that
matters — the Privacy Policy governs.

## 4. Contact

Questions about this notice, or about a child's information, can be sent to
privacy@iniminimo.ai.
