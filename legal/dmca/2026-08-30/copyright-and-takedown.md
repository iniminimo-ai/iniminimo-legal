# Copyright and Takedown Requests

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Iniminimo respects copyright. If you believe material available through our
service infringes a copyright you own or represent, you can send us a notice and
we will act on it.

## What your notice needs to include

To be effective, a notice must be in writing and include all of the following:

1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner, or a person
   authorised to act on their behalf.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work you say has been infringed.
3. Identification of the material you say is infringing, with enough detail for
   us to find it. A direct link is the most useful thing you can give us.
4. Your contact details: address, telephone number and email address.
5. A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorised
   by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
6. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate, and, under
   penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on
   their behalf.

Send it to dmca@iniminimo.ai, or by post to the address above. Email reaches us
faster.

## What we do when we receive one

We review the notice, and where it is valid we remove or disable access to the
material. We notify the person who posted it and give them the notice, and we
tell them they can send a counter notification. We keep a record of notices we
receive.

## Counter notification

If your material was removed or disabled and you believe that was a mistake or
misidentification, you can send a counter notification to the same address. It
must include your physical or electronic signature, identification of the
material that was removed or disabled and where it appeared before removal or
disabling, a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith
belief the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or
misidentification, your name, address, telephone number and email address, and a
statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the U.S. federal district
court for the district where you live, or, if you are outside the United States,
any judicial district in which Iniminimo may be found, and that you will accept
service of process from the person who sent the original notice or that person's
agent. After receiving a valid counter notification, Iniminimo may forward it to
the person who sent the original notice. Unless that person notifies Iniminimo
that they have filed an action seeking a court order to restrain you from
engaging in infringing activity related to the material, Iniminimo may restore
the removed or disabled material in not less than 10 and not more than 14
business days after forwarding the counter notification.

## Repeat infringers

We may terminate or restrict the accounts of users who repeatedly infringe
copyright, in appropriate circumstances.

## A note on misuse

Sending a notice that knowingly misrepresents material as infringing carries
liability under U.S. law. If a video features your own child and you want it
removed, you do not need a copyright notice. Write to privacy@iniminimo.ai and
we will review it.
